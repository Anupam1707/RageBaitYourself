ABOUT THE GAME

RageBait Yourself is a first-person challenge game about movement, misdirection, and the small decisions that turn "easy" into "one more try".

Six stages progress from a dark forest maze and obstacle gauntlet to platforming, relic hunting, an escape room, and a target finale. The world is crafted to feel tempting, readable, and deliberately untrustworthy.
